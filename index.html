✨ What Is techo5-dot?

techo5-dot is a special Linux firmware made for your Echo Dot (2nd generation). It completely removes Amazon's software (Fire OS) and Alexa, giving you full control over your device. Instead of sending your voice recordings to Amazon's servers, everything stays on your own network.

Think of it as a brain transplant for your Echo Dot. You get a smart speaker that works with your own smart home system (Home Assistant), uses all seven microphones to hear you clearly, and can even act as a regular Bluetooth speaker. No cloud, no tracking, no unnecessary internet connection.

🎯 Amazing Features

🎤 All Seven Microphones Working

Your Echo Dot has seven microphones. With techo5-dot, every single one of them works. This means it can hear you from across the room, even with music playing. Perfect for hands-free voice control.

🏠 Home Assistant Voice Satellite

Turn your Echo Dot into a smart home voice assistant that works with Home Assistant. Ask it to turn on lights, adjust your thermostat, or check the weather – all processed locally on your network.

🔊 Bluetooth Speaker Mode

When you're not using voice commands, your Echo Dot becomes a high-quality Bluetooth speaker. Connect your phone or computer and play music, podcasts, or anything else you want.

🔒 Signed Updates & SSH Access

Keep your device safe with signed A/B updates – this means updates are verified and secure. Plus, you get SSH access, so you can log into your Echo Dot like a mini computer and customize it however you like.

🔓 Privacy First, Always

No more sending your voice data to Amazon. With techo5-dot, your conversations stay on your device and your network. It's your hardware, your data, your rules.

🛠️ Common Questions & Troubleshooting

"I can't see my Echo Dot on my computer."

Make sure you're using a data USB cable (not just a charging cable). Try a different USB port on your computer. If you're still having trouble, try restarting your computer.

"The installation failed halfway through."

Don't panic. Just double-click the installation tool again and run it. It will safely continue from where it stopped.

"Can I go back to Alexa?"

Yes! The original Amazon firmware can be restored. Instructions are available on the GitHub page if you ever want to switch back.

"Will this work with my other smart devices?"

Absolutely! If your smart devices work with Home Assistant, they'll work with techo5-dot. It's designed to be the perfect bridge.
